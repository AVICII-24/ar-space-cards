# Bedside lesson plans

One 10 to 15 minute plan per card. Each plan works three ways: with the printed card and camera, in Demo mode, or in 3D with no card at all. Every plan can stop at any step without losing anything.

**Shape of every session**

| Minutes | Step | What the teacher does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settle | Check with the nurse. Wipe card and tablet. Start a session (learner code, never a name). |
| 2 | Wonder | Ask the opening question. Accept any answer. Do not correct it yet. |
| 5–8 | Explore | Child leads: taps the glowing dots, moves the sliders. Teacher asks "What do you notice?" |
| 3 | Explain | Read the card text or mission explanation together at the child's level. |
| 1 | Close | Ask the closing question. End the session and let the child pick a face. |

Levels: **E** Explorer (Grades 3–5) · **N** Navigator (Grades 6–8) · **S** Scientist (Grades 9–12). Change level at any time from the top of the screen.

## 01 Our Solar System

- **Big idea:** Planets orbit the Sun. Nearer planets go round faster.
- **Wonder:** "Which planet do you think has the shortest year?"
- **Explore:** Tap each planet. Slide *Speed* up and watch Mercury lap the others. Press *Today* to see where the planets really are now.
- **Ask:** E: "Which planets are small and rocky?" · N: "How many Earth years is one Jupiter year?" · S: "Tap three planets. Is T²/a³ always close to 1? What does that tell you?"
- **Close:** "If you lived on Neptune, how many birthdays would you have had?"
- **Lab (N, S):** Kepler's laws. Turn on *True distances* and talk about how empty space is.

## 02 Earth

- **Big idea:** Earth spins. Day is the side facing the Sun.
- **Wonder:** "Where is the Sun at night?"
- **Explore:** Find the yellow dots (Indian cities). Slide *Clock in India* from noon to midnight. Tap the axis and the night side.
- **Ask:** E: "Is it day or night in India right now on the globe?" · N: "How many degrees does Earth turn in one hour?" · S: "Why is IST 5 h 30 min ahead of Greenwich?"
- **Close:** "When it is noon here, who is asleep?"
- **Mission:** Day and night.

## 03 The Moon

- **Big idea:** The Moon shines by reflected sunlight. Half of it is always lit.
- **Wonder:** "Does the Moon make its own light?"
- **Explore:** The lighting shows today's real phase. Slide the day of the month. Tap the dark "seas", Tycho crater and Shiv Shakti Point.
- **Ask:** E: "What made the round marks?" · N: "How many Earths would fit between us and the Moon?" · S: "Why do we always see the same face?"
- **Close:** "Look for the Moon tonight. Does it match?"
- **Mission:** Moon phases. With the Earth card too: How far is the Moon?

## 04 The Sun

- **Big idea:** The Sun is a star. Its energy is made in the core.
- **Wonder:** "What is the Sun made of?" Remind the child never to look at the real Sun.
- **Explore:** Find the tiny blue dot (Earth to scale). Tap the sunspot and the prominence. Press *Look inside* and tap the core.
- **Ask:** E: "How many Earths fit across the Sun?" · N: "Why do sunspots look dark?" · S: "Where does the Sun's energy come from? What does E = mc² say?"
- **Close:** "What does India's Aditya-L1 spacecraft do?"
- **Mission (with Earth card):** Seasons.

## 05 Mars

- **Big idea:** Mars is a cold desert world that India has visited.
- **Wonder:** "Why is Mars red?"
- **Explore:** Tap Olympus Mons, the great canyon and the ice cap. Find the two moons. Tap Mangalyaan.
- **Ask:** E: "How many moons does Mars have?" · N: "A 30 kg child feels like how many kg on Mars?" · S: "Why can we only launch to Mars every 26 months?"
- **Close:** "Would you go? What would you take?"
- **Lab (N, S):** Plan a trip to Mars.

## 06 Jupiter

- **Big idea:** Jupiter is a giant ball of gas with its own family of moons.
- **Wonder:** "Could you stand on Jupiter?"
- **Explore:** Tap the Great Red Spot. Tap each of the four moons and watch which goes round fastest.
- **Ask:** E: "Which moon is fastest?" · N: "Which moon has volcanoes? Which hides an ocean?" · S: "Count orbits: how many times does Io go round for one orbit of Ganymede?"
- **Close:** "Why might scientists look for life on Europa?"

## 07 Saturn

- **Big idea:** The rings are billions of pieces of ice, each in its own orbit.
- **Wonder:** "Are Saturn's rings solid?"
- **Explore:** Turn the view to see the rings edge-on and from above. Tap the rings, the dark gap and Titan.
- **Ask:** E: "What are the rings made of?" · N: "The rings are 280,000 km wide and about 10 m thick. What everyday thing is that thin for its width?" · S: "Why do ring particles not clump into a moon?"
- **Close:** "Would Saturn float or sink in water?"

## 08 Space Station

- **Big idea:** People live in orbit, only 400 km up.
- **Wonder:** "How high do you think the Space Station flies?"
- **Explore:** Tap the solar arrays, the modules and the radiators. Notice India below.
- **Ask:** E: "How many sunrises do astronauts see each day?" · N: "400 km is about Delhi to Amritsar. Does that feel near or far?" · S: "Work out g at 400 km. Is it close to zero?"
- **Close:** "Who was the first Indian to stay on the Space Station?"
- **Mission:** Gravity in orbit.

## 09 LVM3 Rocket

- **Big idea:** Rockets drop empty parts to go faster.
- **Wonder:** "Why does a rocket come apart as it flies?"
- **Explore:** Tap each part. Press *Launch*, or slide the timeline and stop at each separation.
- **Ask:** E: "Which parts fall away first?" · N: "What are the three stages, in order?" · S: "Use Δv = vₑ ln(m₀/m₁). Why does dropping mass help?"
- **Close:** "Where in India does it launch from?"

## 10 Chandrayaan-3

- **Big idea:** India landed near the Moon's south pole and drove a rover there.
- **Wonder:** "Why can't a parachute work on the Moon?"
- **Explore:** Tap the ground to drive Pragyan. Tap the lander, the ramp and the Earth in the sky.
- **Ask:** E: "How many wheels does Pragyan have?" · N: "Why did the mission last about 14 days?" · S: "Why might permanently shadowed craters hold ice?"
- **Close:** "What is celebrated on 23 August?"

## The five concept missions

Each mission follows predict → observe → explain. Use the same five prediction questions as the **Concept check** (Teacher tab) before a child's first session and after their last one.

| Mission | Cards | Misconception it targets | The moment to watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day and night | 02 | The Sun moves around the Earth | India crossing the day–night line while the Sun stays put |
| Seasons | 04 + 02 | Summer is when Earth is nearer the Sun | The tilt never changes; Earth is nearest the Sun in January |
| Moon phases | 03 | Earth's shadow causes the phases | The Moon is always half lit; only our view changes |
| How far is the Moon? | 02 + 03 | The Moon is close to Earth | 30 Earths, then all the planets, in the gap |
| Gravity in orbit | 08 | There is no gravity in space | At zero sideways speed it falls straight down |

**If the child's prediction was the common misconception:** this is the best case, not a mistake. Say "lots of people think that" and let the scene do the correcting.

## Adapting for the child in front of you

- **Cannot hold or see a card:** use 3D. Everything is available there.
- **Tires quickly:** do Wonder and Explore only. Finish the mission another day; the badge is kept for the session.
- **Sensitive to motion or busy screens:** turn on Calm mode and turn labels off in Teacher → Set-up.
- **Cannot read yet:** use Read aloud, and talk through the scene in the child's own language.
- **Small group in a playroom:** one child holds the tablet, one moves the card, one reads. Swap for each card.
