# Content review checklist

**Status: not yet reviewed.** The card and mission text was drafted from standard public figures (NASA planetary fact sheets, ISRO mission pages) but has not been checked line by line against those sources. That review should happen before any pilot.

All text lives in two files: `content/cards.js` and `content/missions.js`. The product is English only for now.

## How to review

1. **Science review** (founder or another scientist): read every fact at all three levels. Tick it or correct it in the file.
2. **Translation review**, when Hindi or another language is added (a science teacher who teaches in that language): check accuracy, natural phrasing and age-appropriate vocabulary, especially the Explorer level.
3. **Hospital review** (a paediatric nurse or child-life specialist): flag anything that could upset a child in treatment.
4. Record the reviewers' names and the date at the top of this file.

## What the app computes rather than states

- **Planet positions today** (Solar System card): mean orbital motion from J2000 elements. Good for showing where planets are; not accurate enough for observing.
- **Moon phase today**: mean lunar month, so it can differ from the true phase by up to about half a day.
- **Earth's tilt direction today, sub-solar point, Delhi day length**: simple declination formula, accurate to about a degree.
- **Orbit mission**: real gravity (GM = 398,600 km³/s²) integrated step by step from 400 km altitude.
- **Mars planner**: a true Hohmann transfer between circular orbits (259 days, 44° lead angle). Real orbits are slightly elliptical and tilted.

## Things deliberately left out

- **Country borders.** The Earth shows only land and sea, so there is no boundary depiction to get wrong.
- **Sound effects and timers.** Nothing is sudden and nothing is timed.
- **Names, photos or accounts.** None are collected.
