# Teacher and facilitator guide

For hospital teachers, child-life staff and volunteers. Read once before your first session. The short version is also inside the app under **Teacher → Bedside guide**.

## What is in the kit

- 10 laminated AR cards and one "Start here" card with a QR code
- A tablet (or any phone or tablet with a camera and a recent browser)
- A stand, so the child does not have to hold the tablet
- Disinfectant wipes approved by the ward

## Setting up a tablet (once)

1. Connect to Wi-Fi and scan the QR code on the "Start here" card, or type the app address.
2. Wait on the home screen for a few seconds. The app stores itself on the tablet and then works without Wi-Fi.
3. Add it to the home screen (browser menu → *Add to Home screen*).
4. When the app first asks for the camera, choose **Allow**.
5. Recommended: lock the tablet to the app (Android: *App pinning*; iPad: *Guided Access*) so there are no ads, chat or links.

## The three ways to use it

| Mode | When to use it |
|---|---|
| 📷 Camera | The child can see a card on the tray table. The 3D scene sits on the card. |
| 🧊 3D | The child is lying flat, cannot hold or see a card, or the light is poor. No card needed. |
| 🎬 Demo | Showing the kit to staff, parents or visitors without any cards. |

Switch between them at the top of the screen at any time.

## The three levels

| Level | Grades | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 🔭 Explorer | 3–5 | Plain words, short sentences |
| 🧭 Navigator | 6–8 | Numbers and comparisons |
| 🧪 Scientist | 9–12 | Formulas and extra readouts |

Pick by the child's interest and energy today, not only by age. Tap the level button at the top to change it mid-session.

## Running a session

Follow the in-app guide, or the plans in [LESSON_PLANS.md](LESSON_PLANS.md). The essentials:

1. Check with the nurse. Wipe the card and tablet.
2. **Teacher → Session → Start session.** Enter the ward and a learner code such as `A-07`. **Never type a name.**
3. Let the child choose the card. Let the child lead.
4. Do one mission if there is time and energy.
5. **End session** and let the child pick a face.

**Stop immediately** if the child is tired, dizzy, in pain or upset, or asks to stop. Nothing is lost.

## Using two cards together

- **Sun + any planet card:** the planet is lit from wherever the Sun card is.
- **Sun + Earth (Seasons mission):** slide the Earth card around the Sun card. The date and Delhi's daylight change as it moves.
- **Earth + Moon (Gravity lab):** slide the cards together. The app measures the real gap in centimetres.

## If a card is not recognised

- Tilt the card or tablet slightly to move the glare off the black-and-white square.
- Keep the whole square visible, with fingers off it, 20 to 50 cm from the camera.
- Flatten a bent card. Replace it if the lamination is peeling.
- Or switch to 3D. Everything still works.

## Measuring what children learn

- **Concept check** (Teacher tab): the five prediction questions. Use it **before the first session** and **after the last**, with the same learner code. Do not correct answers during the check.
- **Results** (Teacher tab): sessions, minutes, mood ratings, first predictions and before/after scores.
- **Export CSV**: saves the anonymous results as a spreadsheet file for the pilot report.

## Privacy

- No login and no names. A learner code is something you make up; keep your own paper list if the ward needs one.
- The camera picture is analysed on the tablet and thrown away. It is never saved or sent.
- Results stay on the tablet until you export or erase them (Teacher → Set-up).

## Hygiene

- Wipe cards and tablet before and after every child.
- In isolation rooms, use a card set that stays in that room.
- Do not share a stand between isolation and open wards without cleaning it.
